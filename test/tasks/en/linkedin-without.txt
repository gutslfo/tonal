Big news from Levain: we're starting wholesale in spring 2027. 🥖

For years, we've baked for the people who walk through our doors at 3 Mill Lane. Now we want to bring our bread to more tables across the neighbourhood.

We'll be supplying cafés and restaurants nearby with the same loaves we're proud to sell in our own bakery: slow-fermented, baked fresh, and made with care. Being local means your bread arrives only hours out of the oven, and we can build an order around what your kitchen actually needs.

If you run a café or restaurant close to Mill Lane and would like to hear more, we'd love to talk. Write to us at hello@levain.example and we'll be in touch.

Spring 2027 is a way off, but we're starting conversations now so we can get things right from the first delivery.

Thank you to everyone who has supported Levain so far. This next chapter is because of you.
