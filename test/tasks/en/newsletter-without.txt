Good news for anyone who has ever arrived at Levain mid-afternoon to find the shelves bare: from 1 November, our country loaf will be baked twice a day. The first batch still comes out of the oven at dawn, as it always has, and now a second follows at noon, so there will be a fresh, crackling 800 g loaf waiting for you well into the afternoon. Whether you're a morning regular or a lunchtime wanderer, we'll have one ready for you at 3 Mill Lane.
