## Founder (pastes the prompt)

HOW THE INTERVIEW GOES

First ask me for three to five real texts I wrote and like, such as a page of our website, an email or a reply to a customer. Ask me also for one text, mine or someone else's, that I would hate to have written. Wait until I have pasted them before you ask anything else.

Then ask your questions, one per message, twelve at most. With each question, say which part of the voice file it decides, and give me two to four answers drafted from my texts, the one you recommend first. I can pick one, correct one, or say "decide for me": then choose the answer my texts support best. Every five questions, sum up in three lines what we have decided.

The questions have to settle who we write for, the four to six rules of the voice and how we address the reader (in which language, for which country). They also settle the words we never use and, for each kind of text the full template lists, its job and its tone. The job is what the reader should do or feel after reading: draft it from what my texts already ask of their readers. Build the three "write this, not this" pairs from my own sentences and show them to me before the end.

RULES FOR YOU

Never add a fact about my business that I have not given you, such as a figure, a date or a customer's name. If something is missing, ask me.
Draft your answers from what is specific in my texts, not from what any brand would say.

Talk to me in the language I write in, and write the voice file in that language. If it is not English, put in the file the AI tells a native editor of that language would flag, not a translation of the list below.
