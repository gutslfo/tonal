The Grave Water x Deathgrip Murder Mitts are limited edition black and gold mittens, made with Deathgrip for riding your board or playing in the snow.

They are called Murder Mitts because we wanted your hands to look ready for a crime, and the only victim we have in mind is your thirst. Wear them on the mountain, then take them off and bury a can of Grave Water in the snow so it is ice cold when you come back for it.

Limited edition means that once this batch is gone, it is gone for eternity. Add a pair to your cart before the supply joins the dead.
