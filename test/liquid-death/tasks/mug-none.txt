**Mug of Doom**

Some mugs hold your coffee. This one holds your coffee and a warning.

The Mug of Doom is a tankard-style mug finished in antique silver and covered all over in raised skulls. Every inch of it is packed with grim detail, so there's always something new to find as you drink. The handle is a stack of skulls, giving you a solid, sinister grip.

Fill it with morning coffee, tea, or hot chocolate. Pour in a Grave Water iced tea, sparkling water, or an energy drink and raise it high. Beer, cider, and whatever else you're drinking will do just fine too. The Mug of Doom isn't picky. It only asks that you drink boldly.

**Details**
- Tankard-style mug
- Antique silver finish
- Raised skull design all over
- Handle made of stacked skulls
- For hot or cold drinks

Drink deep. Rest later.
