**Pot Head Planter**

Meet the newest creature to take up residence in your home: the Pot Head Planter from Grave Water.

This bright red pot is shaped like the head of a three-eyed monster, complete with a toothy grin full of fangs. Its glare is unsettling, but it's friendly enough. Plant something leafy in the top and it sprouts like a wild head of hair, so your monster gets a fresh new look as it grows.

Put it on a windowsill, a desk, a bookshelf, or out on the patio. Fill it with trailing ivy, spiky succulents, or a tuft of grass for a messy green mop. Give it water (ideally ours) and a little sunshine, and it will happily guard your space.
