The Pot Head Planter is our newest home and garden item: a red plant pot shaped like the head of a three-eyed monster with fangs. Your plant grows out of the top like hair, which gives the monster a full head of it for eternity or until you forget to water it.

All three eyes will watch your windowsill around the clock, so any plant that dies in there will do so under supervision. If you buy one piece of merch to put a living thing in, make it this one, because the fangs are already in place for when the plant needs protecting.
