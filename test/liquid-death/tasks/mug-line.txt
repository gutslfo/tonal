**The Mug of Doom**

Some mugs whisper "good morning." This one screams it, then buries the body.

Meet your new favorite vessel: a tankard-style mug in antique silver, covered in raised skulls from rim to base. Every inch is crowded with the faces of the departed, so you'll never drink alone. The handle is a stack of skulls too, because even the grip deserves a mass grave.

Fill it with coffee, tea, sparkling water, or any drink you like. Fill it with Grave Water if you want to stay hydrated and sugar-free, which is more than we can say for the previous owners of these skulls.

**Features:**
- Tankard-style mug in antique silver finish
- Raised skulls all over the body
- Handle made of stacked skulls
- Suitable for coffee, tea, or any other drink
- Pairs well with low-sugar hydration and a healthy fear of the inevitable

Drink up. You can sleep when you're dead, and you can hold this mug until then.
