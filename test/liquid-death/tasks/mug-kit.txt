The Mug of Doom is a tankard-style mug in antique silver, covered all over in raised skulls, with a handle made of stacked skulls.

Fill it with coffee or any other drink, and that drink will be served in a vessel fit for a banquet in the underworld. Your hand will rest on a small tower of skulls every time you take a sip, which is the closest any beverage has come to a coronation in our experience.
