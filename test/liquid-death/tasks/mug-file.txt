The Mug of Doom is a tankard-style mug in antique silver, covered all over in raised skulls, with a handle made of stacked skulls. It holds coffee or any other drink, so your morning brew can finally be served with the gravity it deserves.

Every skull is raised, which means you can feel your doom with your fingertips. The handle is a tower of skulls, and you will grip it every time you lift the mug, like a medieval executioner who just wants a refill.

Fill it with Grave Water, iced tea, coffee or whatever else you plan to drink. The skulls will watch over it, and your thirst will not be seen again.

Style: Tankard mug
Finish: Antique silver
Design: Raised skulls all over
Handle: Stacked skulls
Use: Coffee or any other drink
